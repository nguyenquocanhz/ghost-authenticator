Built with Uncompromising Security

Engineered for speed, privacy, and full compliance with RFC 6238 time-based one-time password standards.

AES-256 Bit Encryption

Your master vault is hardened using industry-standard AES-256 and PBKDF2 key stretching. Not even system administrators can read your raw seeds without the master key.

100% Offline & Zero Telemetry

No account required, no remote servers, no trackers, and no background telemetry. What happens on your PC stays strictly on your PC.

Universal RFC 6238 TOTP

Compatible with all major platforms including Google, Microsoft, AWS, Discord, Binance, Steam, Epic Games, Facebook, and thousands more.

QR Code & Drag-and-Drop

Instantly import accounts by dragging and dropping QR code images, scanning with your camera, or pasting standard otpauth:// secret keys.

Native Performance

Crafted in high-performance .NET 8 WPF for Windows 10 and 11. Lightning-fast launch speed with ultra-low RAM footprint.

Adaptive Fluent Themes

Seamlessly synchronizes with Windows system theme settings, offering high-contrast dark and light modes for maximum clarity.

Transparent Security Architecture

Security through transparency, following open standards and proven cryptographic primitives.

Cryptographic Assurance: Ghost Authenticator utilizes System.Security.Cryptography with PBKDF2 (SHA-256, 100,000 iterations) for key derivation and AES-256 GCM for authenticated envelope encryption. Plaintext secret keys are wiped from memory immediately after TOTP calculation.
