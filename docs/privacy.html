Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026 | Last Updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ghost Authenticator ("we", "us", or "our"), developed by Nguyen Quoc Anh, handles information when you use our Windows desktop application available through the Microsoft Store and GitHub.

Key Privacy Guarantee: Ghost Authenticator is an offline-first desktop tool. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal data, passwords, 2FA secret keys, or usage telemetry to any external servers or third parties.

1. Information We Process

Ghost Authenticator processes the following data exclusively on your local device:

  • Two-Factor Authentication (TOTP) Secret Keys: Base32-encoded seed keys and account labels (e.g., username or issuer service like Google, GitHub, Microsoft) that you manually provide or import via QR code.
  • Master Encryption Password: If configured, your chosen master password is used in real-time with PBKDF2 to derive an encryption key. We never store your master password in plaintext anywhere.
  • User Preferences: Local interface settings such as light/dark theme preference, window size, and view configurations.

2. How Data is Stored and Protected (Security)

In strict compliance with Microsoft Store Policy 10.5.4 (Security of Personal Information):

  • All saved authenticator accounts and secret keys are encrypted at rest using AES-256 (Advanced Encryption Standard in GCM mode) combined with PBKDF2 key derivation (SHA-256 with 100,000 rounds).
  • Data is stored locally in your private Windows user profile directory (e.g., %APPDATA%\GhostAuthenticator or isolated MSIX package storage).
  • No cloud synchronization or unencrypted remote backups take place unless you explicitly export an encrypted backup file to a destination of your own choosing.

3. Device Capabilities & Permissions

Ghost Authenticator requests only the minimal system capabilities necessary for its core utility:

  • Camera / Webcam (Optional): If you choose to scan a physical 2FA QR code with your webcam, video frames are decoded in memory exclusively by the open-source ZXing engine. Video feeds or snapshots are never recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted over the network.
  • Screen Capture / Drag & Drop (Optional): Used only to extract QR code data from an image file or selected screen coordinate that you explicitly provide.
  • Network Capabilities: Ghost Authenticator operates completely offline. The application does not contain background analytics, advertising trackers, or external crash-reporting SDKs.

4. Third-Party Sharing and Disclosures

Because Ghost Authenticator runs locally on your PC and operates without a central server:

  • We do not share, license, or sell your data to advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.
  • We do not track user behavior across other applications or websites.
  • We do not integrate with third-party tracking services or ad networks.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

You have full, absolute control over your information at all times:

  • Access & Export: You can view and export all your 2FA accounts and tokens directly within the application settings.
  • Permanent Deletion: You can delete individual accounts or choose "Wipe All Data" inside the application. Uninstalling the application via Windows Settings or Windows Store automatically removes all locally stored application container files.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

In accordance with Microsoft Store Policy 10.5.8 and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), our application is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in our software or regulatory standards. Any revisions will be published with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp on this page.
